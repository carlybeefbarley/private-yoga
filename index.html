Let's talk

Yoga that meets you where you are.

Your body isn't exactly like everyone else's, so your yoga practice doesn't have to be either.

Private sessions give us the time and space to talk about what you're looking for and create a practice that makes sense for you.

I'm Carly. I've been teaching yoga for about six years, in studios, gyms, schools, private sessions, workshops, and with sports teams. Most of my teaching has been vinyasa, but I've also taught yin, slower practices, sculpt, and arm balance workshops.

My own relationship with movement extends beyond yoga, and I bring that curiosity into how I teach. I'm less interested in making a body fit a particular shape than in paying attention to the person in front of me—what feels good, what doesn't, what they're working toward, and what might actually be useful that day.

Why private yoga?

Group classes have to work for a room full of people. Private yoga gives us the freedom to focus on you.

  • Work toward a specific goal or skill
  • Address areas of tightness, weakness, or discomfort
  • Build confidence if group classes aren't your thing
  • Develop a sustainable home practice
  • Move at a pace that feels right for you

Sessions & pricing

Private sessions are designed around what you want from your practice. Sessions can take place in your home or another suitable private space.

60 minutes — $150

90 minutes — $200

Travel within Seattle city limits is included. Sessions on the Eastside and in nearby areas may include an additional travel fee, typically around $25 depending on location.

All you need is enough space to move comfortably and a yoga mat if you have one. I can bring any additional props we need. No previous yoga experience is necessary.

Reduced-rate sessions

I want private yoga to be accessible to people who might not otherwise be able to afford it. A limited number of sessions are available at 80% of the standard rate if the regular rate would make private yoga financially inaccessible to you. No documentation required.

Good to know

Payment is due on the day of your session. Cash, Venmo, PayPal, and Cash App are accepted.

Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations within 24 hours are charged the full session rate. I understand that emergencies happen, and those can be handled individually.

Practice beyond the mat.

Yoga asks us to practice ahimsa—to move through the world with compassion and a commitment to non-harming. I believe that practice extends beyond the mat.

A portion of my private-session income is donated to organizations supporting people affected by displacement, violence, and injustice.
