Yoga that meets you where you are.

Your body isn't exactly like everyone else's, so your yoga practice doesn't have to be either.

Private sessions give us the time and space to talk about what you're looking for and create a practice that makes sense for you.

Why private yoga?

Group classes have to work for a room full of people. Private yoga gives us the freedom to focus on you.

  • Work toward a specific goal or skill
  • Address areas of tightness, weakness, or discomfort
  • Build confidence if group classes aren't your thing
  • Develop a sustainable home practice
  • Move at a pace that feels right for you
